Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the billing cycle? ▼

We bill you every 4 months (120 days) so you can plan ahead and take advantage of longer billing periods with peace of mind.

Can I cancel my subscription? ▼

Yes! You can cancel anytime from your account settings. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period.

What if I want to downgrade? ▼

Cancel anytime and your account will revert to Free tier at the end of your billing period. All your invoices and data remain accessible.

Is my payment information secure? ▼

We use industry-standard encryption and tokenization through Stripe. Your payment details are never stored on our servers—they're secured by Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure.
